# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss code and evidence; do not attack people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and deliberate exposure of private information are not welcome.

Accept constructive correction and make room for different levels of experience. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation, explaining decisions when appropriate without revealing private reports.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through the hosting platform. This project has not yet published a separate reporting address.
